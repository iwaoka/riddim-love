Privacy

Analytics

RIDDIM.LOVE uses Google Analytics 4 (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how the site is used — for example which pages are visited and how people search. This uses cookies and similar technologies provided by Google. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.

Advertising storage, ad personalisation and ad data sharing are turned off.

Your choice

Visitors from the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked first: analytics cookies are only set after you choose “Accept”. Elsewhere, analytics runs by default.

To change your choice, clear this site's data (cookies and site storage) in your browser; you will be asked again on your next visit. You can also use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

More about how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

What we do not do

There are no accounts, and RIDDIM.LOVE does not ask for or store personal details. Your search target choice is kept only in your own browser.

Data

Riddim, tune, artist and label data is provided by Riddim-ID.
